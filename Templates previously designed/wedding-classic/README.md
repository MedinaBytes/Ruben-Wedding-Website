# Wedding · Classic — Aurelia & Theodore

Flagship of the new **events/** series. A late-summer, lakeside wedding invitation
website with an editorial, classical-italic feel.

## Concept
- Couple: Aurelia Bellanti & Theodore Maren
- Date: September 14, 2026
- Venue: Villa Camellia, Tremezzo (Lake Como, Italy)
- Tone: refined, quiet, warm — black-tie optional, not stiff

## Palette
- Ink `#1F1A14`
- Ivory `#FAF5EC` / Ivory 2 `#F1E9D8`
- Antique gold `#B89766` / deep `#8d7048`
- Dusty rose `#D9AFA4`
- Sage shadow `#6E7A60`

## Type
- Display: **Cormorant Garamond** (italic for names)
- Body: **Jost**

## Sections
1. Hero — names, date, live countdown, "Respond by" CTA
2. Our Story — vertical timeline (4 milestones)
3. The Day — schedule cards (6 timings)
4. Venue — photo + meta + map links
5. Travel — three partner hotels + dress code with palette swatches
6. Wedding Party — 4 cards with portraits
7. Gallery — masonry-ish grid (7 photos, asymmetric spans)
8. Registry — 3 cards (honeymoon, charity, traditional)
9. FAQ — 6 disclosure rows
10. RSVP — full form (validates client-side, no backend)
11. Footer — monogram, hashtag, nav

## Run locally
Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or from the repo root: `npx serve . -l 3000`
then visit `http://localhost:3000/events/wedding-classic/`.
