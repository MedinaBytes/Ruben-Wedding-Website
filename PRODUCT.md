# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Invited guests open a private link, often on a phone, to understand the wedding day, find both venues, choose a language, RSVP, and optionally suggest songs. Ruben and Andrea use a separate authenticated admin area to manage invitations and responses.

## Product Purpose

Create a personal digital invitation for Ruben and Andrea's wedding in Vienna. The guest experience makes the celebration feel personal while keeping date, arrival time, venue, travel, RSVP, and music details clear. The couple can manage invitation and attendance data without exposing the guest list publicly.

## Brand Personality

Intimate, elegant, playful, and personal. The invitation should feel warm and considered, with humor reserved for the explicitly approved moments in the wedding brief.

## Anti-references

Do not reproduce the visual identity, exact composition, copy, assets, or code of the five previous wedding templates. Do not make the experience feel like a generic wedding theme, a marketing funnel, or an admin dashboard presented to guests. Do not expose private guest or home information.

## Design Principles

- Make the practical details effortless to find and act on.
- Treat each invitation as a personal welcome, not a generic announcement.
- Use real couple photography and wedding-specific botanical details to carry the story.
- Keep guest data private and collect only what is needed to organize the celebration.
- Make every interaction usable with keyboard, touch, assistive technology, and reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, clear form labels and errors, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and mobile touch targets. Never use color alone to convey important information.
